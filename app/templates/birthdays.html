Put birthdays on your phone

Subscribe once and new birthdays show up by themselves.

iPhone · Subscribe Android · Google Calendar

Step-by-step help

How to add it on an iPhone

If the iPhone · Subscribe button doesn’t work, do it by hand:

  1. Copy the calendar link below.
  2. Open the Settings app. Tap Apps, then Calendar. (On older iPhones, just tap Calendar.)
  3. Tap Calendar Accounts → Add Account → Other.
  4. Tap Add Subscribed Calendar, paste the link into Server, and tap Next.
  5. Tap Save. The birthdays now show up in the Calendar app.

Use the Google Calendar app on your iPhone? Follow the Android steps.

How to add it on Android (Google Calendar)

The Google Calendar app can’t add a link by itself, so you do it once on the website:

  1. Copy the calendar link below.
  2. Open Chrome and go to calendar.google.com. If it says to use the app, tap ⋮ and check Desktop site.
  3. Next to Other calendars, tap +, then From URL.
  4. Paste the link and tap Add calendar. Leave “Make the calendar publicly accessible” unchecked.
  5. In the Google Calendar app: ☰ → Settings, tap the new birthday calendar, and turn on Sync.

New birthdays can take up to a day to appear. That’s Google’s schedule.
