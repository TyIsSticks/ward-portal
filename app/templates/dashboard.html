How to add it on an iPhone

Easiest: on your iPhone, tap this button, then tap Subscribe, then Add.

Subscribe on iPhone

If the button doesn’t work, do it by hand:

  1. Tap Copy above to copy the calendar link.
  2. Open the Settings app. Tap Apps, then Calendar. (On older iPhones, just tap Calendar.)
  3. Tap Calendar Accounts → Add Account → Other.
  4. Tap Add Subscribed Calendar, paste the link into the Server box, and tap Next.
  5. Tap Save. The birthdays now show up in the Calendar app.

Use the Google Calendar app on your iPhone instead? Follow the Android steps.

How to add it on Android (Google Calendar)

The Google Calendar app can’t add a calendar link by itself, so you do it once on the Google Calendar website. It then appears in the app automatically.

Easiest: tap this button, sign in to Google if asked, and tap Add.

Add to Google Calendar

If that opens the app instead, or doesn’t work, do it by hand:

  1. Tap Copy above to copy the calendar link.
  2. Open Chrome and go to calendar.google.com. If it says to use the app, tap the ⋮ menu and check Desktop site.
  3. On the left, next to Other calendars, tap +, then From URL.
  4. Paste the link and tap Add calendar. Leave “Make the calendar publicly accessible” unchecked.
  5. Open the Google Calendar app. Tap ☰ → Settings, tap the new birthday calendar, and turn on Sync.

New birthdays can take up to a day to show up. That’s Google’s schedule, not yours.
